Loading & Editing Songs

Survey the song, pick the notes you need, and arrange parts, pitch, and length. Instruments and volume are covered in Sound & automation.

First checks after loading

Loading MIDI splits parts from the original tracks and channels. NBS splits by original layers and instruments. Parts from different source tracks are never merged, even on the same instrument.

  1. Check title and BPM.
    The header shows the song title and current BPM. Confirm it is the song you meant.
  2. Press FIT.
    Fit time and pitch on screen to survey where the notes are.
  3. See the part structure on the ALL tab.
    Use the color-coded notes and part tabs to grasp melody, backing, bass, and drums.
  4. Play it roughly.
    Press Space for a local preview and check nothing sounds extreme.

MIDI instruments are guessed from General MIDI numbers plus track and instrument names. In songs with a sustain pedal, notes extend to where the pedal lifts, not where the key lifts (extended counts are shown). Dense tempo changes are simplified into editable points.

NBS supports format v0–v6 and keeps layer volume and pan. Non-audio control notes such as “Tempo Changer” and “Sound Stopper” are excluded.

Notes outside the vanilla range

The piano roll keeps original pitches and marks out-of-range areas with a distinct background. Preview and submission fold them into Minecraft's playable range.

Fine-tune with the Note Inspector

Edit selected notes from the inspector on the right. With multiple notes selected, edits apply relative to the primary note.

  • Start & length: choose milliseconds or note values from 1/1 to 1/64.
  • Pitch: −1/+1 moves a semitone, −12/+12 moves an octave.
  • Instrument, volume, pan: change the selection together (see instruments & sounds).
  • Detune: offsets pitch in cents from the placed note.
Example: raise only the melody by one octave

Open the melody part, box-select the passage with Shift+drag, and press +12 in the inspector. Only the selection moves up an octave; press −12 to undo if it sounds too high.

Choosing snap

Each press of the SNAP button cycles 1/4 → 1/8 → 1/16 → 1/32 → 1/64 → OFF → 1/4 (S does the same). Use 1/4 or 1/8 for large structural moves and 1/16–1/64 for ornaments. OFF allows free placement but drifts easily, so verify by listening.

Organize notes into parts

Parts group notes by tone or role. Keep the original split or reorganize it in OMMT.

Create a part from the selection

  1. Select the notes to split off.
  2. Press NEW PART FROM SELECTION in the Note Inspector.
  3. Open the new part and adjust its instrument and name.

Move to an existing part

  1. Select the notes to move.
  2. Pick the destination in Move selected to.
  3. Press MOVE TO EXISTING PART.

Rename a part

Activate the part, type a name into “Active part name”, and press RENAME ACTIVE PART.

Example: split one piano part into “Melody” and “Backing”

Box-select the upper melody and split it with “New part from selection”. Rename the new part “Melody” and the original “Backing”. Changing only the melody's instrument and volume clarifies each role.
