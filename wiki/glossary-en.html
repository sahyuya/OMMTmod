Glossary

A short reference for terms used in the editor and this handbook.

On this page
  • Notes, parts, and layers
  • Bars, BPM, and snap
  • Pan, range, and automation

Notes, parts, and layers

Note
One sound with a pitch, start time, duration, volume, and other properties. Shown as a bar in the piano roll.
Part
A group of notes in OMMT, useful for editing one role such as the melody.
Layer / track
A grouping in an NBS / MIDI source file. After import, you can organize these notes into OMMT parts.

Bars, BPM, and snap

Bar / measure
A group of beats used to locate a passage, numbered above the piano roll.
BPM / tempo
Playback speed. A higher BPM is faster.
Snap
Aligns note moves and resizes to rhythmic divisions.
Playhead / seek
The current playback position, and the action of moving to a different position.

Pan, range, and automation

Pan
The left-to-right position of a sound; 0 is centered.
Pitch range
The range of playable pitches. A displayed source pitch may sound different when playback is fitted to vanilla range.
Automation
Graphical editing of properties such as volume or tempo. OMMT has separate volume, pan, tempo, and release panels.
Cents / detune
Fine pitch offset. 100 cents equals one semitone.
